    ## Establish a Charitable Giving Program

    Many businesses encourage – even incentivize – employees to take part in charitable giving programs. For the sake of convenience, some companies offer charitable paycheck deductions and match a certain amount of funds to charities on a company-approved list. Atlanta Care Center is a 501(c)3 nonprofit and can be added to your company’s charitable giving program.

    For more information on starting a charitable giving program, [contact us](/contact/).

    ## Life Change Campaign

    Your business or your organization can host a “Life Change Campaign” fundraiser for our center. In this easy, creative campaign, you encourage employees and visitors to donate spare change in an empty baby bottle. We will bring everything you need to begin. This campaign can make a surprising impact on our center. A one-week Life Change Campaign once brought in nearly $6,000 to our center!

    For more information on how to begin, [contact us](/contact/).

Your business plays a significant role in helping our center serve women in Atlanta with pregnancy services. Thanks to your business’ continued support, we provide women with holistic care and [services](/how-we-serve/), including:

- Pregnancy Testing
- Limited Ultrasounds
- Support Care Plans
- Patient Advocacy
- Maternal Support (Newborn, Infant, and Maternity Clothing)
- Registered Nurse Consultations
- Factual, Evidence-based Pregnancy Decision Support
