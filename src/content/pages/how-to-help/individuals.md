    To make a difference, there are some things we all can do, but is there something unique that only you can do?     ## Volunteering Your Time

    We are always looking for compassionate and dedicated people to volunteer to serve our patients. If you would like to volunteer, we would be happy to explore the commitment with you.

    Whether it’s serving as a Patient Advocate, organizing our Clothes Closet, or assisting with data entry, we have a place for you to get involved. [Contact us](/contact/) and get involved today!

You play a significant role in helping our patients in Atlanta make life-affirming choices. Without your faithful support, we could not provide our patients with [holistic services](/how-we-serve/), including:

- Pregnancy Testing
- Limited Ultrasounds
- Support Care Plans
- Patient Advocacy
- Maternal Support (Newborn, Infant, and Maternity Clothing)
- Registered Nurse Consultations
- Factual, Evidence-based Pregnancy Decision Support
