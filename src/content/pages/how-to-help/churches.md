    Our center appreciates the regular, monthly sustaining investment of like-minded churches and organizations that allows us to serve pregnant women in Atlanta. Your financial commitment to the center is what makes it all possible.

We are a pregnancy center dedicated to helping women make positive choices. Thanks to the support of local churches, we can provide women with holistic care and [services](/how-we-serve/), including:

- Pregnancy Testing
- Limited Ultrasounds
- Support Care Plans
- Patient Advocacy
- Maternal Support (Newborn, Infant, and Maternity Clothing)
- Registered Nurse Consultations
- Factual, Evidence-based Pregnancy Decision Support
