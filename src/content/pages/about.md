    ## Why We Serve Atlanta

    More abortions are performed in Atlanta than in any other city in Georgia. We serve in Midtown Atlanta because Atlanta and surrounding zip codes have the greatest need.

    We relentlessly meet the needs of our patients with free of charge services and support, including:

    - Pregnancy Testing
    - Limited Ultrasounds
    - Support Care Plans
    - Patient Advocacy
    - Maternal Support (Newborn, Infant, and Maternity Clothing)
    - Registered Nurse Consultations
    - Factual, Evidence-based Pregnancy Decision Support

    With your financial support, we continue to offer realistic and hope-filled alternatives to thousands of Atlanta women. Atlanta Care empowers pregnant mothers to choose life with confidence.
